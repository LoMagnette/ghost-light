Voxxy, Droid and Biggy drive through one surveyed model of the Kinepolis Antwerp across three eras, from an empty hall to a conference at capacity. The game client is a three.js-rendered, DOM-UI single-page app built on a home-grown, mass-aware physics core; a family of Node command-line checks verifies the simulation, the venue geometry and the built game without a human having to play it end to end. See SPEC.md for the design brief and CLAUDE.md for build conventions.
